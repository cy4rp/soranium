measurements. They are not measurements of block inclusion or mining
throughput. The earlier synthetic STAS measurements were removed when the
wallet path moved to the official DSTAS SDK.

## Official DSTAS benchmark status

The 10,000-token official DSTAS benchmark was not run. The local wallet had
only 325,694 sats across its remaining P2PKH UTXOs, with most outputs being
1-sat benchmark change outputs; it therefore could not provide the required
10,000 independent fee UTXOs and issue funding. The old synthetic STAS
benchmark numbers must not be reused as official DSTAS results.

`broadcastTps` in every retained benchmark means Arcade HTTP 202 acceptance
throughput, not block inclusion or mining throughput.
